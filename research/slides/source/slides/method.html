The model

Popularity plus item neighbours, re-ranked by LambdaMART

Popularity

accounts per token, last 14 days (the baseline)

Item neighbours

tokens traded by the same accounts (cosine, k = 400)

Fixed blend

0.5 × popularity + 0.5 × neighbours, each scaled per account

LambdaMART ranker

re-orders ~680 candidates with 20 features

Top 10

tokens per account, every 2 days

Without the neighbour features the ranker falls back to popularity level (recall 0.105 against 0.101): neighbours carry the gain.
