Now your task is the following, take the idea: 

# Idea: the first seconds of a memecoin launch, bundles, copycats and outcomes

And do the following workflow:

1. Literature review
- Inside research/literature/ review, download the relevant litreture for the idea.
- Go through each of the articles, summarize them.
- Gather all their insights.
- Think about how these insights can be applied to our crypto idea at hand. And how it affects our idea.
- Create a literature review .md file and put all your information about the insights here.

2. Data
- Look for the required data.
- Create a ipynb notebook.
- Download the needed data into data/ directory.
- Analyse and validate the assumptions about the data. Is the data useful, is there enough, is relevant for the idea.
- Look at the descriptive stats.
- What would be the feature set, what would be the labels?
- Do the sizes look good?

3. Preprocessing and split
- In the notebook perform exploratory data analysis with the aim of uncovering some preprocessing that is needed.
- If the data looks usuable, create the train/dev/test splits.
- Look out for data leakage.

4. Method fit
- Does the proposed method fit our task and data?

5. Baseline
- create a fixed baseline for our task
- evaluate its performance
- what metrics do you use and why?
- what is the evaluation protocol?

6. Create a writeup
- How does the baseline perform?
- What do we expect from applying more complex models?
- What are your conclusion about the dataset, the baseline, the method we want to use?
- Is there opportunity, potential in this idea?
